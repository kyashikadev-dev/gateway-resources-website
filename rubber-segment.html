Rubber – Segment

Used Rubber

Responsible Recycling Solutions

Rubber mainly pertains to end-of-life tyres from automobiles, commercial vehicles and tractors. Waste rubber has been a subject of deliberation for nations as it is a culprit of illegal dumpings, non-biodegradability and environmental havoc, despite being an integral part of any economy. Gateway Resources is directly involved in the procurement and supply of such materials to responsible recyclers in that utilise it to create an economically sound and environmentally safe output.
